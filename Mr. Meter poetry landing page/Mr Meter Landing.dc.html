Meet Mr. Meter

He's a poet, and he's genuinely curious about your people.

Talking to him feels like sitting in a studio with someone who has all afternoon. He asks one question at a time. He tells you a small thing about himself. He never asks about poetry — only about your person.

What he keeps is the specific stuff: the half-octave in her voice, the bag pressed to her chest. That's what ends up in the poem, in your words rather than prettier ones.
